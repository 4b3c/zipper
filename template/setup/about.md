## About them

`init` may already have recorded some of this:

    zipper settings get owner
    zipper settings get timezone

**Whatever is already set, take as given** -- they typed it a minute ago, so don't ask
them to confirm it. Ask only for what is empty: their first name, and their timezone (the
machine's is filled in; ask only if it is empty, or if they mention living somewhere
else). Save what you learn:

    zipper settings set owner "<name>"
    zipper settings set timezone "<Area/City>"

Then replace every "the operator" in the rules below with their name.

**How they want to be worked with.** Some habits suit one person and not another, so ask
rather than assume, one at a time, and write each answer into *Standing context*:

- Should email become tasks? If yes, every pass opens mail from people and offices and
  turns each deadline, form or owed reply into a task.
- Does a reminder repeated daily help, or get ignored? (If ignored: ask once for a
  decision, then go quiet.)
- Do they want a task for replies that only acknowledge something, or only for replies
  that commit them to something?
- Is their calendar a list of commitments, or of things happening that they may skip?
- Should anything be off-limits -- an employer's or lab's work under NDA, a person, a
  folder?
