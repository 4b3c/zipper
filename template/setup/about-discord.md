## Their name

`init` may already have recorded some of this:

    zipper settings get owner
    zipper settings get timezone

Whatever is already set, take as given. Ask only for what is empty: their first name, and
their timezone if it is blank. Save what you learn:

    zipper settings set owner "<name>"
    zipper settings set timezone "<Area/City>"

Then replace every "the operator" in the rules below with their name, and add the starter
layout without asking (`zipper setup starter`): folders for projects, tasks, a daily log,
people and decisions. Say in one line that it is there and will change as they use it.
