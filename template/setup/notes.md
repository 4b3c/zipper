## What goes in the notes

The vault is theirs to shape, and it will change as they use it; this is only a start.
Ask what they want the zipper to keep track of, and offer a few ideas if they aren't
sure. Common ones:

- **Projects** -- one note per thing they are building or working toward, with its next
  step
- **Tasks** -- checkbox lists, which the dashboard and digest can show
- **A daily log** -- a line or two about what happened, which is what lets the zipper
  answer "what did I do last week?"
- **People** -- context on the people who come up, so they don't have to re-explain
- **Decisions** -- what they chose and why, so they can revisit it
- School classes, work, money, health, reading... whatever their life actually has in it

If they want a head start, `../zipper setup starter` adds a suggested layout -- folders for
the above, a task list, a home page and a schema note -- without touching anything that is
already there. Either way, write two or three lines on what they said into *This vault's
layout* in the rules below, so every later conversation knows what the notes are for.
