## Gmail

Offer this to anyone who said email should become tasks. Once connected, each pull lists
new mail as rows (who, the subject, a pointer, never the body), and every pass opens mail
from people and offices and turns each deadline, form or owed reply into a task.

It is read-only: the zipper can read their mail and never send it. Say so, because the
Google screen will ask about reading mail. Then:

    zipper scoop enable gmail
    zipper google --discord

The second command prints a link and the steps to send with it, and posts a button. Send
both and end your turn. When they paste the address, a `[secret] ...` message says whether
Google is signed in. If it is not, it says why; run `zipper google --discord` again for a
fresh link, because each code works once and only for a few minutes.

Two things they may hit: Google warns that the app is unverified (expected: Advanced, then
continue), and a school or work account may block it outright. Then a personal Gmail is the
way, or forwarding that mail to one.

`zipper scoop guide gmail` says how to work the rows once mail arrives. The first pull is
a baseline: mail already there is not news, so it adds no rows.
